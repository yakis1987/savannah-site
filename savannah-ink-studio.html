The Shop

FINE ART Tattooing

Savannah Ink is a fine art tattoo shop on Bull Street in historic Savannah, Georgia. With a team of three artists - Peter, Frankie, and Brandi - the shop has built a reputation for precision, originality, and work that holds up for decades.

The shop is known for its welcoming atmosphere that puts both first-timers and seasoned collectors at ease. Every consultation is taken seriously, and no design leaves without the artist's full creative investment.
