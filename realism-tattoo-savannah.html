The Style

WHAT IS REALISM tattooing?

Realism tattooing attempts to replicate photographs, painted portraits, or real-world subjects with photographic accuracy on skin. At its best, it's technically indistinguishable from photography at a glance. No style rewards — or punishes — artist selection more than realism.

In Savannah, Drop Dead Tattoo has produced award-winning realism work and is the city's top destination for dark realism and portrait pieces. Savannah Ink Tattoo also has realism-capable artists. For both shops, a consultation is mandatory before booking — realism requires a completely custom approach to every piece.

What to Know

HOW TO DO IT right

01

ARTIST SELECTION IS EVERYTHING

Only book a realism artist who has a demonstrated history of realism work — not just 'can do realism.' Look at healed portfolio work, not just fresh photos.

02

CONSULTATIONS ARE MANDATORY

Realism requires reference photos, size planning, and placement discussion. Never walk in for a portrait piece.

03

SIZE DETERMINES DETAIL

Photorealistic detail requires space. A portrait that looks amazing at 6 inches will become unrecognizable at 2 inches. Bigger is always better for realism.

04

HEALED WORK IS THE REAL TEST

Ask to see healed portfolio work at least 6–12 months old — that's the real test of an artist's execution.

05

AVOID SUN RELIGIOUSLY

Realism depends on subtle tonal gradations that fade quickly in direct sun. Lifelong sun protection is the price of a great realism tattoo.

06

BUDGET SERIOUSLY

A quality portrait starts at $400–$600 and scales up significantly. See our how much does a tattoo cost in Savannah for what to expect.

How long does a realism tattoo take?

A single portrait typically takes 4–8 hours. Larger or more complex pieces take multiple full-day sessions.

No. Realism requires a consultation, custom reference preparation, and advance planning. Always book ahead.
