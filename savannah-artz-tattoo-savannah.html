About the Artist

Meet Savannah Artz

Yes, Savannah Artz is her real name. And it suits her perfectly. A resident artist at Dollhouse Tattoos in Savannah, Georgia, she has been specializing in custom tattooing for over three years, but has been drawing since she could hold a crayon.

Her path to tattooing was unexpected: she was studying psychology when a shop owner noticed her artwork online and reached out. From that moment, she dedicated herself completely to the craft and never looked back. Today she channels her lifelong drive to create into every piece, pushing herself to do better from one tattoo to the next.

Her process is intentional: every design is kept private until the appointment, ensuring that first reactions are genuine. It's an approach that has earned her a loyal following of clients who travel from across the country to sit in her chair.
