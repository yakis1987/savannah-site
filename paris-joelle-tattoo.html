The Artist

PARIS Joelle

Paris Joelle is a Savannah-based tattoo artist specializing in custom fine line, black & grey realism, color work, and memorial pieces. Located on Bull Street in the heart of Savannah, her shop is known for its clean, welcoming environment and a deeply personalized approach to every tattoo.

Paris believes tattoos are more than ink - they're visual representations of personal journeys, milestones, and the people we carry with us. Every design is created exclusively for the person wearing it.
