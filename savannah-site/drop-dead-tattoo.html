The Shop

AWARD-WINNING Since 2023

Drop Dead Tattoo is Savannah's most ambitious newer shop - opened in 2023 and already award-winning. Located on Abercorn Street in South Savannah, the shop runs a large team of artists covering virtually every tattoo style, from abstract and graphic to realism and fine line.

What sets Drop Dead apart is their commitment to accessibility - open 7 days a week from 10am to 10pm, with genuine walk-in availability even late in the evening.

The shop runs regular flash events where artists offer custom designs at special prices - a great way to get tattooed by talented artists on a budget. Drop Dead is deeply rooted in the Savannah community, hosting charity events like "Tattoos for the Paws" and other local initiatives that give back to the city they call home.
