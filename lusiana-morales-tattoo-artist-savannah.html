About the Artist

WHY Lusiana

Lusiana Morales is a fine artist and tattoo artist based in Savannah, GA. She holds an MFA in Fibers and BFA in Painting from SCAD — bringing graduate-level fine arts training to every design she creates.

Her work is rooted in the natural world: botanicals, plant forms, organic compositions, and the quiet detail of living things. Every piece carries the sensibility of a trained painter — considered line weight, intentional composition, and an eye for what lasts.

Training & Background

CREDENTIALS & Experience

✦ SCAD

Lusiana holds a Master of Fine Arts in Fibers from the Savannah College of Art and Design — one of the top art schools in the country. Her graduate work explored natural materials, organic forms, and the interconnections of the natural world.

— MFA in Fibers, SCAD

✦ Painting

Her BFA in Painting from SCAD gives her a foundation in composition, color theory, and line that most tattoo artists simply don't have. It shows in every piece she creates.

— BFA in Painting, SCAD

✦ Apprenticeship

Before SCAD, Lusiana completed a formal tattoo apprenticeship — so her technical tattooing skills are as solid as her artistic background. Years of experience as a working tattoo artist.

— Formal Tattoo Training
